# Final captions

**Figure 6.** Evolution of precipitation and 500 hPa circulation from 18 to
20 June 2023. Columns show 18, 19 and 20 June (BJT). Panels (a–c) show MSWEP
daily precipitation and ERA5 absolute 500 hPa geopotential height; (d–f) and
(g–i) show Hot17 and Cold17 composites for the 12 June initialization; (j–l)
show Hot17−Cold17 differences. Members follow the predefined Total-period Tmax
groups. Shading shows precipitation (mm day⁻¹); contours show absolute height
(gpm) in (a–i) and height differences (gpm) in (j–l). Blue dashed and red solid
contours denote negative and positive height differences. Dotted hatching in
(j–l) marks local two-sided Welch p < 0.05 for precipitation. Z500 significance
is not displayed.

**Figure S6.** As in Figure 6, for the 8 June initialization and its own
Total-period Tmax Hot17/Cold17 members. The full observation row (a–c) uses
the same dates and observations as Figure 6. Both figures share absolute and
difference precipitation color levels.

**Figure 7.** Hovmöller diagrams of daily precipitation and 500 hPa geopotential
height during the Total period, averaged over 35°–44°N. The window is 14–24
June 2023 (BJT), with the retained cosine-latitude-weighted mean. The centered
shared observation panel (a) combines MSWEP precipitation and ERA5 absolute
Z500. Left/right forecast columns represent 8/12 June initializations:
Hot17 (b/c), Cold17 (d/e), and Hot17−Cold17 (f/g). Groups use each
initialization's Total-period Tmax membership. Vertical reference lines mark
114°E and 119°E. Absolute and difference precipitation scales are shared.
Z500 contours are absolute in (a–e) and differences in (f–g), with blue dashed
negative and red solid positive difference contours. Dotted hatching in
(f–g) marks precipitation local two-sided Welch p < 0.05, tested using
member-level Hovmöller fields. Z500 significance is not displayed.

**Figure 9.** Total-period precipitation and 500 hPa circulation composites
for the 1, 5, 8 and 12 June 2023 initializations, in that row order. Columns
show each initialization's Total-period Tmax Hot17, Cold17, and Hot17−Cold17
fields. The average window remains 14–24 June (BJT). Precipitation is period
mean daily precipitation (mm day⁻¹); Z500 is absolute geopotential height
(gpm) in the composite and observation reference panels, and Hot17−Cold17
height difference in the difference panels. Negative/positive differences
use blue dashed/red solid contours. Dotted hatching marks precipitation local
two-sided Welch p < 0.05 after member-level period averaging. Z500 significance
is not displayed. The original observation reference panel and panel sequence
(a–m) are retained.

All differences are group contrasts, rather than climatological anomalies.
Archived independent Z500 tests and any BH-FDR results remain available for
traceability; the displayed precipitation criterion is local Welch p < 0.05.
