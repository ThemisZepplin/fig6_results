# Captions for the revised main figures

**Figure 6.** Precipitation and 500-hPa circulation averaged over 18–20 June 2023
for ECMWF S2S forecasts initialized on 1, 5, 8, and 12 June. Rows correspond to
the four initialization dates, and columns show the Hot17 composite, Cold17
composite, and Hot17 minus Cold17 difference, respectively; panels (a–l) are
numbered from left to right and top to bottom. The 17 members in each group are
selected separately for each initialization using North China mean Tmax averaged
over the Total period (14–24 June), and the same membership is used for the
18–20 June averages. Shading denotes mean daily precipitation (mm day⁻¹).
Contours denote absolute 500-hPa geopotential height (gpm) in the composite
panels and its Hot17 minus Cold17 difference in the difference panels. Red solid
and blue dashed contours denote positive and negative height differences;
the configured difference levels are ±10, ±20, ±40, ±60 and ±80 gpm, with levels
outside the available field range omitted. Black contours in absolute panels
are spaced by 40 gpm, and the 5880-gpm contour is highlighted in purple where
present. Stippling denotes precipitation differences with local two-sided Welch
p < 0.05, using the 17 member-level three-day means in each group.
Panel (m) shows MSWEP precipitation and ERA5 geopotential height averaged over
the same three days. Dashed boxes mark North China (35–44°N, 114–119°E).
Dates follow the Beijing-time calendar.

**Figure 7.** Hovmöller diagrams of daily precipitation (shading; mm day⁻¹) and
500-hPa geopotential height (contours; gpm) over 14–24 June 2023, averaged over
35–44°N using the established latitude-averaging procedure. Panel (a) shows
MSWEP precipitation and ERA5 geopotential height once, centered above the
forecast panels. Left and right columns show forecasts initialized on 8 and
12 June, respectively: Hot17 in (b,c), Cold17 in (d,e), and Hot17 minus Cold17
in (f,g). Group membership follows each initialization's Total-period mean Tmax
ranking. Red solid and blue dashed contours in the difference panels denote
positive and negative height differences. Stippling denotes local two-sided
Welch p < 0.05 for precipitation differences. Vertical dashed lines mark
114°E and 119°E. Dates follow the Beijing-time calendar.

SI daily panels for 8/12 June retain their existing scientific content; final
supplementary figure numbers remain to be assigned. The former 14–24 June
four-init mean map is removed from the main text but is not deleted.
