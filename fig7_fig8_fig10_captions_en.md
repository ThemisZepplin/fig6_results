# Final English Captions: Figures 7, 8, and 10

## Figure 7

**Figure 7.** Daily evolution of precipitation and 500-hPa geopotential height on 18–20 June 2023 Beijing Time (BJT) for the ECMWF S2S forecast initialized on 12 June 2023. Columns correspond to 18, 19, and 20 June, and rows show the observed fields, the predefined Total-period Hot17 composite, the predefined Total-period Cold17 composite, and Hot17 minus Cold17, respectively. Shading denotes precipitation (mm day⁻¹): MSWEP in the observed row and ECMWF S2S in the ensemble-composite rows. Black contours in the observed and absolute-composite rows denote absolute ERA5 and S2S 500-hPa geopotential height (gpm), respectively; the purple contour marks 5880 gpm. Red solid and blue dashed contours in the bottom row denote positive and negative Hot17-minus-Cold17 Z500 differences. The dashed box marks the North China (NCHN) region (114°–119°E, 35°–44°N). Stippling marks precipitation differences with uncorrected local *p* < 0.05 from a two-sided Welch independent-samples *t* test, and thickened Z500 contours mark locations satisfying the corresponding variable-specific local criterion. Precipitation and Z500 were tested separately using the 17 members in each predefined group; differences are Hot17 minus Cold17.

## Figure 8

**Figure 8.** Time–longitude evolution of precipitation and 500-hPa geopotential height from 14 to 24 June 2023 BJT for the ECMWF S2S forecast initialized on 12 June 2023. Panels show (a) observed fields, (b) the predefined Total-period Hot17 composite, (c) Hot17 minus Cold17, and (d) the predefined Total-period Cold17 composite. Shading denotes precipitation (mm day⁻¹), from MSWEP in panel (a) and ECMWF S2S in panels (b)–(d). Contours denote absolute ERA5 or S2S Z500 (gpm) in panels (a), (b), and (d), and the Hot17-minus-Cold17 Z500 difference in panel (c). All fields are cosine-latitude-weighted means over 35°–44°N. Orange date labels identify 14–17 June and blue date labels identify 18–24 June. The vertical dashed lines at 114°E and 119°E mark the western and eastern longitude boundaries of the NCHN reference region; they are not stage boundaries. Stippling marks precipitation differences with uncorrected local *p* < 0.05 from a two-sided Welch independent-samples *t* test, and thickened Z500 contours mark locations satisfying the corresponding variable-specific local criterion. Precipitation and Z500 were tested separately using the 17 members in each predefined group at each time–longitude cell; differences are Hot17 minus Cold17.

## Figure 10

**Figure 10.** Total-period precipitation and 500-hPa geopotential-height composites for ECMWF S2S forecasts initialized on 1, 5, 8, and 12 June 2023 and verified over 14–24 June 2023 BJT. Rows in the 4 × 3 main block correspond to initialization dates, and columns show the predefined Total-period Hot17 composite, Cold17 composite, and Hot17 minus Cold17. Panel (m) shows the observed Total-period reference fields. Shading denotes precipitation (mm day⁻¹): ECMWF S2S in the main block and MSWEP in panel (m). Black contours in the Hot17, Cold17, and observed panels denote absolute S2S or ERA5 Z500 (gpm); red solid and blue dashed contours in the difference column denote positive and negative Hot17-minus-Cold17 Z500 differences. The dashed box marks the NCHN region (114°–119°E, 35°–44°N). Stippling in the difference panels marks precipitation differences with uncorrected local *p* < 0.05 from a two-sided Welch independent-samples *t* test, and thickened Z500 contours mark locations satisfying the corresponding variable-specific local criterion. Tests were conducted independently for each initialization and separately for precipitation and Z500 using member-level Total-period means from the 17 members in each predefined group; differences are Hot17 minus Cold17.
